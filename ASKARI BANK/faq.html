Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our business banking services.

Account Questions

You can open a business account by scheduling a consultation with one of our relationship managers. You'll need your business formation documents, EIN, and identification. We can guide you through the process in person or virtually.

Our basic business checking has no minimum balance requirement. Premium accounts have varying requirements based on the services included. Contact us for details on specific account types.

Yes, all deposit accounts are FDIC insured up to $250,000 per depositor. For businesses with larger deposits, we offer expanded FDIC coverage through our network of partner banks.

Lending Questions

We offer term loans from $100,000 to $25 million, lines of credit from $50,000 to $10 million, and SBA loans up to $5 million. Equipment financing and commercial real estate loans are available for qualified borrowers.

Most loan decisions are made within 24-48 hours. Funding typically occurs within 5 business days for standard loans. SBA and commercial real estate loans may take longer due to additional requirements.

Generally, you'll need 2-3 years of business tax returns, recent financial statements, business plan (for startups), and personal financial statements for guarantors. Our team will guide you through the specific requirements.

Treasury Management

Our treasury suite includes cash concentration, zero balance accounts, lockbox services, ACH origination, wire transfers, positive pay, and comprehensive reporting. We also offer investment solutions for excess cash.

Yes, we offer integrations with major ERP systems including SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, QuickBooks, and Sage. Custom API integrations are available for enterprise clients.
