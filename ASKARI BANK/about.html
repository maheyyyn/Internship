A Legacy of Trust & Financial Excellence

Since 1991, Askari Bank has been a leading financial partner in Pakistan, committed to serving over 1.8 million customers with integrity and innovation.

Our Story

Established in 1991, Askari Bank Limited has grown into a premier commercial bank headquartered in Islamabad. Following our acquisition by the Fauji Foundation in 2013, we have expanded our reach to over 650+ branches nationwide.

We take pride in our diverse banking solutions, ranging from conventional retail and corporate services to our specialized Askari Ikhlas Islamic Banking division, which operates 140+ dedicated Shariah-compliant branches.

Today, we lead the way in digital transformation. With over 620,000 active mobile app users and a focus on SME and agricultural banking, we are empowering the economic future of Pakistan.
